# VOC panel: training-set leakage audit

Generated by the audit that produced `train_leakage_audit.json`
(streaming scan of all 335 `data/covid/train/group_*_anc_aa.fasta`, 81,271 leaves).

A leaf "carries the bundle" if its residues match the VOC's `defining_bundle`
at `bundle_min_hits` positions (from `benchmarks/voc_threat_panel.json`).

## The origin-country cases are leaked

These six were in `cases_manifest_primary.json`. Their trees are *in
`data/covid/train`* — the model was trained to generate these exact leaves from
this exact root, so any "recovery" is at least partly memorization.

| case | bundle carriers inside that training tree |
|---|---|
| Delta g191 (India) | 114 / 299 leaves |
| Beta g302 (South Africa) | 19 / 299 leaves |
| Alpha g323 (UK) | 2 / 206 leaves |
| Lambda g289 (Peru) | 1 / 163 leaves |
| Omicron BA.1 g309 (South Africa) | 0 in-tree, 774 elsewhere in train |
| Mu g034 (Colombia) | 0 in-tree, 330 elsewhere in train |

They now live in `cases_manifest_leaked.json` and must not be used for a
recovery claim. They are still useful as a **memorization ceiling**: an upper
bound on recall when the target genuinely was in training.

## What the held-out cases can and cannot claim

`cases_manifest_clean.json` (Gamma g003, Delta g007, Delta g011) is Brazil
geo-test. Those trees were never seen. But the Gamma bundle appears in 232
training leaves from other countries, and Delta's in 1321. So the defensible
claim is **held-out population**, not **unseen mutation**:

> The model never saw this Brazilian lineage, and reproduces its VOC signature.

not

> The model had never seen K417T/E484K/N501Y.

## The standard we actually apply

Shared mutations between training and target are fine and in fact expected — the
model is supposed to learn high-escape trajectories, and those reuse the same
sites across populations. The requirement is narrower: **the model must never
have seen this root or this tree.** Under that standard the Brazil geo-test cases
pass and the six origin cases fail, because their trees were literally training
targets.

That does not need a retrain. It needs new roots, built from accessions that
never entered a training tree — which is what `data/covid_voc_roots` is.

## New unseen roots (no retrain)

`download_sars2_voc_roots_ncbi.py` pulls NCBI genomes by Pango lineage for each
VOC-origin population, excluding all 110,122 accessions already placed in a
`data/covid/{train,val,test}` tree. Verified overlap: **0**.

The single most useful finding from the country inventory: the training corpus
covers Africa, Asia, Europe, Oceania and South America only. **North America is
entirely absent — zero USA sequences.** So USA roots are unseen at the population
level, not merely the tree level, and the USA is where Epsilon (B.1.427/429,
California) and Iota (B.1.526, New York) emerged.

| population | VOC | seqs pulled | target-lineage | strength |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| usa_epsilon | Epsilon | 1550 | 800 | country absent from training |
| usa_iota | Iota | 900 | 400 | country absent from training |
| uk_alpha | Alpha | 900 | 400 | unseen root; country has 206 train seqs |
| south_africa_beta | Beta | 588 | 400 | unseen root; country in train |
| colombia_mu | Mu | 575 | 400 | unseen root; country has 29 train seqs |
| india_delta | Delta | 150 | 18 | thin; GenBank holds little Indian data |
| peru_lambda | Lambda | 40 | 33 | too few for a tree |

GenBank is far poorer than GISAID for South Africa, India and Peru, so those
panels are thin; Peru drops out entirely. Beta and Mu survived only after the
completeness filter was corrected (NCBI marks 29.5 kb genomes `PARTIAL`, so the
gate is now length, not the completeness flag).

Trees are temporally stratified across each emergence window rather than chunked
by date, so the inferred root predates the variant and the leaves contain it —
otherwise there is nothing to forecast.

## A retrain would only be needed for a stricter claim

Only if a reviewer demands that the VOC's *mutations* were also unseen, which is
a stronger bar than the one above and arguably the wrong one for this model.
Two candidate VOC-blind splits, neither currently scheduled:

**A. Pre-VOC temporal cutoff.** Train only on sequences collected before
2020-10-01, i.e. before Alpha/Beta/Gamma/Delta emerged. 13,891 of 86,524 dated
train sequences qualify (16%); 32 existing train trees already end before that
date, more if trees are re-chunked under the filter. Every VOC in the panel is
then genuinely unseen — mutation, bundle, lineage and country. Mirrors
EVEscape's "pre-pandemic data only" framing.

**B. Bundle-filtered global split.** Keep all dates, drop the 109 training trees
containing any VOC-bundle carrier, and additionally hold out the origin
countries (ZA, GB, IN, PE, CO). Leaves 226 training trees. Retains far more data
and sequence diversity, but the guarantee is per-bundle rather than universal,
and near-miss variants at the same sites survive in training.

Caveat that applies to both: the reference process uses a pretrained pLM, whose
corpus is not date-controlled. State this explicitly rather than claiming a
fully pre-2020 information set.

## Also held out, and useful as roots

`data/covid/val` is Australia — 29 trees, almost all Jan–Sep 2020, none carrying
a VOC bundle (`australia_screen.md`). Excellent *prospective roots* under either
retrain. Not usable as observed targets for Gamma/Beta/Mu/Lambda, since those
lineages never circulated in Australia at meaningful frequency.
